nocturne but better 2.6.1 (formerly nocturne flat scroll)
windows x64 | steam nocturne 1.0.1 | tested steam build 25487568
works with bepinex 6 (bundled) or melonloader 0.7.3 and newer (not bundled)

install
1. install nocturne through steam on the other pc, then close the game.
2. extract this entire zip to a folder. do not run it from inside the zip.
3. double-click install.cmd. it finds the steam game folder automatically.
   if asked for a folder, use steam > nocturne > manage > browse local files
   and paste the folder containing nocturne.exe.
4. start nocturne normally through steam. the first launch can take longer
   while the loader prepares files; internet access may be needed.
5. open options > gameplay and set:
   note scrolling   - default, 2d downscroll, or 2d upscroll
   receptor height  - -10% to +30%; moves the receptors in from their edge
                      of the screen in both 2d modes (0% = original spot)
   note size        - 50% to 150%; notes and receptors in both 2d modes
   lane spacing     - 60% to 150%; how far apart the 2d lanes are
   note skin        - default bars, circles, or arrows
   note flares      - on or off; the burst when you hit or hold a note
                      (mine explosions always show)
   timing bar       - off or on; shows how early (rabbit) or late (turtle)
                      each hit was, next to the receptors
   timing bar position - 2d upscroll only: below enemy (under the receptors)
                      or above enemy (along the top of the screen)
   enemy attack opacity - 0% to 100%; how visible the enemy stays while it
                      attacks (100% = unchanged)
   infinite consumables (arcade) - off or on; in arcade battles, using a
                      consumable doesn't use it up. you still get one use
                      per battle, and battles that set your gear use
                      their own.
   the game's own note colors row gets a preview under it, with a red mine
   next to your notes so you can spot palettes that look like it.
6. in options > audio, under sound effects:
   hit sound        - off or on; a short tick when you hit a note
   hit sound volume - 5% to 100%; the game's sound effect volumes apply too
   miss sound       - on or off; the same setting as note miss sounds
   miss sound volume - 10% to 300%; 100% is the game's normal level

which loader gets used
- melonloader 0.7.3 or newer installed and turned on: the mod goes into mods.
- no melonloader: the bundled bepinex loader is added where missing and the
  plugin goes into bepinex\plugins.
only one copy runs at a time. to switch loaders, run install.cmd again; it
disables the copy for the other loader. do not keep bepinex and melonloader
in the same game folder, because only one of them can start.

custom battles and the arcade button (new)
the main menu gets an arcade button. it needs a save, like continue does,
and opens the arcade on your latest save. arcade scores go into that
save's .score file; your place in the story and your .sav files don't
change. custom battles are arcade-only battles with your own song and
charts, in the arcade's custom tab. make them in the battle creator:
custom charts on the main menu (or the custom charts tab in options) >
battle creator. its pages:
   info          - title, artist, charter, lore, and the arcade card's
                   picture: the card's slot is square, so fill it or show
                   the whole picture, crop it, pick crisp pixels, and see
                   an exact preview
   song          - an ogg, mp3, wav, flac, m4a or wma file
   charts        - six difficulties in 4 or 5 lanes, in the chart editor
   enemy         - a game enemy's fight with your own stats and info boxes
   art           - your own enemy art: image, gif, video or sprite sheet.
                   "turn into frames" makes a video a sprite sheet, so a
                   see-through colour works on it
   gear & level  - set gear or the player's own, and a set level or the
                   player's own. the player's own gear and level come
                   back after the battle, and the arcade shows what a
                   battle sets before you play it
   dialogue      - boss-style lines before the song, during it (over the
                   notes, or stopping the song), after a win and after a
                   loss.
                   speakers: karma (the player), game characters with
                   their faces, the narrator, or your own with pictures
a battle exports and imports as one .nbbbattle file, and deleting one
moves it to the recycle bin. a new battle can also start from an
osu!mania .osz (beta, not recommended). technical-notes.md describes
battle.json and the folder layout.

test in the chart editor (new)
the chart editor's test button (f5 from the playhead, shift+f5 from the
start) plays the chart in a real battle and brings you back to the
editor. it works for custom battles and the game's songs when the editor
was opened from the title screen, and nothing is saved.

fixed in 2.6.1
- after quitting a fight from the pause menu, later fights had no music
  and their notes stuttered back and forth.
- battles with an #offset (charts timed with "first beat here", most osz
  imports) played their notes off the music and started with notes
  already partway down the lane. the offset now counts, and when the
  first note comes early the notes start up to 3 s before the song.
- custom enemy art stuck on its defeat picture once the enemy's health
  hit 0, and the attack opacity setting stopped fading its attacks.
- an error in options > gameplay when the custom charts page hid the row
  it had selected.
your battles don't need any changes.

fixed in 2.6.0
2.5.0 garbled the results screen's high-score info in every battle. 2.6.0
records custom chart scores without the hook that caused it.

custom difficulties and chart editor
press custom charts on the main menu, or the custom charts tab in options:
   chart editor         - make or edit a chart for any song, with the song's
                          music playing, like osu!mania's editor: tools,
                          bookmarks, metronome, scroll speed changes, the
                          enemy's events, and keys you can change
   battle creator       - make or edit custom battles (see above)
   import custom chart  - opens a windows file picker; pick a .nbbchart
                          pack or an .sm file
   custom chart song    - the songs you have custom charts for
   custom difficulty    - off, or the chart to play instead of the game's
                          chart on that song, whatever difficulty you pick
   export custom charts - saves this song's or all songs' picked charts
                          into one .nbbchart file to share
   custom chart folder  - open it, or write the game's own charts into it
the difficulty screen also gets a custom entry under zen. the song's enemy
events stay in, and custom chart scores are saved apart from the game's high
scores. boss fights split into parts (m1 to m3, gauntlets 1 to 4) can't take
custom charts yet. the readme explains the editor.

chapter badges
in arcade and high scores, a chapter button turns gold with two sparkles
once every encounter in it has its sparkles.

the flat modes include compact, vertical health/energy bars. alt, tab, and
the windows key don't start a battle at "press any key". any other key or a
controller button still does.

installations start on default, 0%, 100% size and spacing, default skin,
note flares on, timing bar off and below the enemy, enemy attack opacity
100%, infinite consumables off, hit sound off at 80%, and miss sound volume
100%. your selections are saved on that pc. the title screen and the
intro's logo card read "nocturne but better, by terrydavisgaming".

optional fullscreen flicker fix
if you have the flickering black bars, close nocturne and double-click
enable-fullscreen-fix.cmd. this applies the same directx 11 preference used
on the original pc. restore-fullscreen-fix.cmd reverses that change.
the scrolling mod works independently of this fix.

remove
close nocturne, then run uninstall.cmd to disable this mod for both loaders.
run restore-fullscreen-fix.cmd too if you enabled the display fix.
uninstall keeps the loaders because other mods may use them. saves, scores,
other mods, custom charts, custom battles, and saved scrolling preferences
are left in place.

if something does not work
- access denied: right-click the relevant .cmd and run as administrator.
- wrong build or unknown files: stop and use a package updated for your
  game version. the installer refuses unknown game files on purpose.
- an existing bepinex conflicts: this package needs bepinex il2cpp x64
  build 788. do not replace another mod setup without checking it first.
- melonloader too old: update it to 0.7.3 or newer, then run install.cmd.
- options rows missing: check bepinex\logoutput.log or
  melonloader\latest.log in the game folder. confirm the zip was fully
  extracted before running install.cmd.
- a custom battle is missing from the arcade: open it in the battle
  creator to see its problems. the loader's log (bepinex\logoutput.log or
  melonloader\latest.log) says why the arcade skipped it.
- an mp3, flac, m4a or wma song won't play on a windows n edition: install
  the media feature pack, or convert the song to .ogg or .wav.
- steam updates / verify integrity may remove the optional display patch.
  this package must be checked again after a game update.

share
send the original nocturne-flat-scroll-2.6.1-windows.zip to the other person
or device. they need their own installed copy of nocturne. the package
contains no game assets, game-generated assemblies, saves, or account data.
it does not support macos, linux, or steam deck through these installers.

contents
- payload: the bepinex plugin, the melonloader mod, and the unchanged
  official bepinex loader archive
- source: mod source and a project that builds either loader's dll
- third-party-notices.txt and licenses: loader notices and licenses
- third-party-source: source archives for the bundled lgpl components
- sha256sums.txt: package file integrity hashes
- technical-notes.md: compatibility, verification, custom battle files,
